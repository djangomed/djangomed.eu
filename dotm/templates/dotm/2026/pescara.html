🏖️ About Django on the Med

Django on the Med is a small, focused development sprint event. It brings people together for three days to work on Django and make progress on challenging issues in the core framework and its ecosystem.

Experienced contributors and people making their first contributions are both welcome. The aim is to provide time, shared space and support for working together.

🧭 Getting to Pescara

✈️ By plane

Pescara Abruzzo Airport has direct connections with several European countries, including Albania, Belgium, the Czech Republic, Germany, Malta, Poland, Romania, Spain and the United Kingdom, with some seasonal variations.

From outside Europe, the easiest options are flying to Rome and taking a direct bus from Fiumicino Airport, or flying to Milan and continuing by domestic flight or train.

🚆 By train

Pescara is well connected by fast trains to Bologna, Milan, Turin, Venice, Bari, Lecce and Bolzano, as well as by direct routes from Rome.

From abroad, Milan and Bologna can be reached directly by train from countries including France, Switzerland, Austria and Germany, before continuing to Pescara.

There is also a historic railway between Rome and Pescara. It is slower, but offers a scenic route through the mountains.

🚌 By bus

There are frequent long-distance bus connections, including direct routes from Rome and Fiumicino Airport, as well as from other Italian cities.

🚗 By car

Pescara is easy to reach through the motorway network, including a direct motorway from Rome. Renting a car can also be useful if you would like to explore the surrounding area.
